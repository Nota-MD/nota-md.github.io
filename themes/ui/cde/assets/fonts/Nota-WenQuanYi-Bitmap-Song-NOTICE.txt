Nota WenQuanYi Bitmap Song
==========================

This TTF is generated directly from the complete WenQuanYi Bitmap Song
16px PCF, preserving both the ASCII and CJK 16x16 bitmap glyphs and embedding
them as EBDT/EBLC strikes for use as the CDE interface and document font.

Original glyph project:

  WenQuanYi Bitmap Song 1.0 RC1
  Copyright (C) 2004-2010, WenQuanYi Project Board of Trustees and
  Qianqian Fang
  https://sourceforge.net/projects/wqy/files/wqy-bitmapfont/
  http://wenq.org/

TTF transcription used by Nota:

  AmusementClub/Wenquanyi-Bitmap-Song-TTF
  https://github.com/AmusementClub/Wenquanyi-Bitmap-Song-TTF

The distributed files are:

  Nota-WenQuanYi-Bitmap-Song-EBDT-16px.ttf
  Nota-WenQuanYi-Bitmap-Song-EBDT-16px.woff2

The WOFF2 is a lossless container conversion of the TTF above. It preserves
all cmap entries, EBDT/EBLC bitmap strikes and naming records; the TTF remains
the editable source form.
