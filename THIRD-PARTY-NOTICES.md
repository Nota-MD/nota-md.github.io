# Third-party notices

## xz-decompress 0.2.3

The theme converter uses the browser build of
[xz-decompress](https://github.com/httptoolkit/xz-decompress) to open installable
GTK themes distributed as `.tar.xz`.

License: MIT. The bundled build also contains xzwasm by Steve Sanderson (MIT),
xz-embedded by Lasse Collin and Igor Pavlov (public domain), and walloc by
Igalia, S.L. (MIT). The complete upstream notice is retained at the top of
`assets/vendor/xz-decompress.min.js`.
